# 3D CAD / GLB Equipment Models Directory

Place optional high-detail `.glb` / `.gltf` 3D industrial equipment models in this folder:
- `d-717.glb` — LP Storage Drum (`D-717`)
- `p-717.glb` — LP Circulation & Transfer Pump (`P-717A/B`)
- `m-761.glb` — Drum Flaker (`M-761A/B`)
- `zz-761.glb` — Lump Crusher (`ZZ-761A/B`)
- `fd-762.glb` — Screw Conveyor (`FD-762A/B`)
- `e-770-c-770.glb` — Vent Gas Condenser (`E-770`) & Vent Gas Blower (`C-770`)

When external `.glb` files are absent, `src/components/three/FlakerUnitScene.tsx` automatically renders procedural Three.js industrial geometry with live operational status indicators.
